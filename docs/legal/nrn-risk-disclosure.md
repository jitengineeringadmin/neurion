# NRN Risk Disclosure

- **No guaranteed value or return.** NRN is a utility token; its value may go to zero.
- **Testnet phase.** MVP runs on Hardhat/Sepolia testnet; testnet tokens have no monetary value.
- **Smart-contract risk.** Bugs may cause loss; contracts are audited before mainnet.
- **Regulatory risk.** Treatment may change; production gated behind compliance sign-off.
- **No custody.** Loss of wallet keys = permanent loss of access. Neurion cannot recover keys.
- **Reward variability.** Rewards depend on verified compute, reputation and demand.
