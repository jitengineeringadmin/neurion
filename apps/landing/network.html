The network, live.

Every point is a machine the public entry node can route to. No addresses, no names, no fingerprints — only how many, and how much is moving. What you are looking at is the shape of the thing, not a register of the people in it.

contacting the network…

–machines reachable through the index
–"who has what" notes held for others
–peers seen recently
–machines answered for, behind closed doors
What this page will not show you. Not one address, not one identity. The peer protocol tells a PEER what it needs in order to route; a web page needs none of that, and publishing it would make the network trivially harvestable. The numbers are aggregates from one entry node — it sees the part of the network it can reach, which is not the whole of it, and there is no central place that could know the whole of it. That is the point.
In italiano. Ogni punto è una macchina raggiungibile attraverso l'indice pubblico. Nessun indirizzo, nessun nome, nessuna impronta: solo quante sono e quanto si muove. I numeri vengono da un nodo d'ingresso, che vede la parte di rete che riesce a raggiungere — non tutta. E non esiste nessun posto che possa vederla tutta: è esattamente lo scopo.
