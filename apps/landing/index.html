What is Neurion

Three things, one network.

🧠

Use AI

Chat and an agent that actually works on your files and projects — locally or routed across the network. Privacy-first routing: sensitive data stays put.

⚡

Share & earn

Your idle machine becomes a compute node. It runs jobs in an isolated sandbox, passes verification and earns NRN. More honest work, more reputation.

🔒

Verified & private

Every sampled computation is re-executed and compared: cheaters are slashed, not paid. Classified privacy and trust-level routing.

How it works

From download to earning.

Download the client

A standalone app: database, local AI and UI included. No Docker, no cloud setup. Double-click and go.

Use it or run a node

Use the AI from the app, or switch on node mode to serve compute to the network from your GPU/CPU.

Pay / earn the verified

You only pay for verified work; as a node you earn convertible NRN. Network state settles on-chain on Base.
