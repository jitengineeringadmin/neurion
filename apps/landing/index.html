What is Neurion

Three things, one network.

🧠

Create with AI

Chat, a Claude-style Code workspace on your folders, image generation and short AI videos with music or voice-over — locally, offline, free. The network steps in when you want more power.

⚡

Share

Your machine passes the models it already has to whoever needs them, and can run work for other people. No payment, no account — the same idea that made file sharing work.

🔒

Verified & private

Every sampled computation is re-executed and compared: cheaters are slashed, not paid. Classified privacy and trust-level routing.

How it works

From download to a network.

Download the client

A standalone app: database, local AI and UI included. No Docker, no cloud setup. Double-click and go.

Use it, or share it

Use the AI from the app. If you want to, pass the models you already have to whoever needs them and lend your processor — both are switches you set, with limits you choose.

Verified, not trusted

Every model is checked against the publisher's own hash, so a file from a stranger either is the real thing or is thrown away.

The hash decides · not the peer
No server in the middle · a distributed index finds who has what
Signed updates · with a key that never touches the server
Your machine, your limits · upload caps, and your router is asked first
