The philosophy

AI you own. That no one can switch off.

If tomorrow OpenAI or Anthropic pull the plug, what do you do? With Neurion the power is yours — and, shared, it belongs to everyone.

🔒

Yours

It runs on your PC. Your data never leaves it. No cloud account, no subscription, no one looking over your shoulder.

🔌

Unkillable

Open weights, already released — impossible to claw back. Fully offline. No company can deprecate, price out or switch off what runs on your own machine.

🤝

Everyone's

When your machine isn't enough, you borrow power from your peers — not from a corporation. Shared, the power is all of ours.

What is Neurion

Three things, one network.

🧠

Create with AI

Chat, a Claude-style Code workspace on your folders, image generation and short AI videos with music or voice-over — locally, offline, free. The network steps in when you want more power.

⚡

Share & earn

Your idle machine becomes a compute node. It runs jobs in an isolated sandbox, passes verification and earns NRN. More honest work, more reputation.

🔒

Verified & private

Every sampled computation is re-executed and compared: cheaters are slashed, not paid. Classified privacy and trust-level routing.

How it works

From download to earning.

Download the client

A standalone app: database, local AI and UI included. No Docker, no cloud setup. Double-click and go.

Use it or run a node

Use the AI from the app, or switch on node mode to serve compute to the network from your GPU/CPU.

Pay / earn the verified

You only pay for verified work; as a node you earn convertible NRN. Network state settles on-chain on Base.
